How songs, melodies and lyrics are found
  • Search uses the Apple iTunes catalog (Deezer as a backup) for the song's title, artist, album, length and a 30-second preview.
  • Timed lyrics come from LRCLIB, a free community lyrics database, matched by title, artist and song length.
  • MIDI is looked up on BitMidi. These are fan-made files, so their tempo and intro may not match the recording. Use the melody offset buttons to line them up, or make the melody from your own MP3 instead.
  • Make melody from audio runs on your computer. Quick traces the strongest pitch in the vocal range. Neural uses Spotify's Basic Pitch model (downloaded on first use) and then keeps the most prominent line. Both follow vocals best when the voice is loud in the mix or isolated.

You can also write a chart by hand as text: one note per line as start note length [lyric], with optional header lines.

# title: Happy Birthday
# bpm: 90
# units: beats        (or seconds, the default)
# offset: 1.25        (seconds of intro before beat 0)
0     G4  0.75  Hap-
0.75  G4  0.25  py
1     A4  1     birth-
2     G4  1     day
